Contact BeeWarm about fireplaces, heating, hot water or gas-fitting work at your property. Include the city, equipment make and model if known, service history and the reason for the enquiry. Robert confirms the scope and availability directly.

Coordinate a strata fireplace service day

Councils and property managers can discuss a coordinated fireplace-service block. Current estimates are $140 each for 1–4 fireplaces, $120 for 5–9, $100 for 10–19 and $85 for 20 or more, before tax. Group rates require tight same-property bookings approved by Robert.

Prepare a booking enquiry

BeeWarm’s public fireplace planning page helps owners and organizers collect the information needed for a service discussion. The staff booking app is in private beta; a public enquiry is not an instantly confirmed appointment. Automatic customer confirmations and online payment are not connected.

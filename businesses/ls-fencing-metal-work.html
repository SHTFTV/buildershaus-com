Plan the perimeter around the property

LS Fencing & Metal Work handles enquiries for chain link fencing, gates, ornamental and cedar fencing, welding and related metal work. Its published service area covers the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland.

Explain access as well as the fence line

Share the property location, approximate fence length, intended height, photos and the purpose of the boundary. For gates, explain the vehicles using the entrance, available space, slopes and any access-control requirements. Confirm measurements and the proposed system with the business.

Strata and commercial coordination

For a shared property, identify who can approve the work and describe resident, delivery and waste-collection access. Ask about repair versus replacement, staging and the work included in the quote. The business website includes service information and project examples for a more specific conversation.

Project enquiries go directly to LS Fencing & Metal Work. Share your location and scope, then confirm availability and the proposed work.
